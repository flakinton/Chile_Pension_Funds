Read before using the numbers

Market value (PV) of derivative positions held by the funds. Bars show local and foreign books where the Superintendencia splits them; the September 2025 and 2026 points cover foreign interest-rate derivatives only.

FX derivatives book

Rates derivatives book

Mostly receive-fixed US$ interest-rate swaps (funds gain if US rates fall), plus local Cámara (CLP) swaps.
